Status
Student project — Design Studio 1

A single ribbon,
wound into a house.

A first-semester exercise in generating a whole house from a single move: one continuous curved wall, spiraling inward from the site's edge to loosely bind the public rooms upstairs and coil down to a subterranean garage below. Glazing follows the curve rather than cutting against it, so the study became as much about how a wrap-around window reads a hillside as about the plan itself.

Student project — Design Studio 2

Classrooms wrapped
around a shared yard.

A studio brief to design a two-storey school as a full loop around a shared courtyard. Repetitive classroom bays line the perimeter, linked by open-air corridors and stairs that spill down onto the yard. The courtyard became the school's shared room — every classroom, stair and corridor opens onto it, turning circulation itself into the social heart of the building.

Student project — Design Studio 2

A house set inside
its own geometry.

A residential studio project pairing a stone-and-stucco house with a landscape designed as carefully as the plan: a grid of square lawns, hedged in low white borders, radiating out from a central dining court. Lighting was treated as a material in its own right — grazing the stone facade, tracing the boundary walls, and marking every path underfoot after dark.
